# Usability test protocol (no results yet)

**Participants:** 3–5 independent filmmakers, producers, sales agents or festival programmers. No incentives promised.
**Format:** 20 min remote, screen share, think-aloud; participant drives the public URL. No login.

Tasks (observe, do not help):
1. "Within 10 seconds, tell me what this tool does." (comprehension)
2. Run the showcase. "Which three markets would you approach first, and why does Territory think so?" (evidence legibility)
3. "Your German buyer just passed. Show me what changes." (War Room discoverability; time to find it)
4. "Cut the localization budget by 40%." Then: "Which changes came from cultural evidence and which from cost?"
5. Edit the brief with a film you know; run it. "Is anything here wrong or surprising?"
6. Export the brief. "Would you share this with a co-producer or sales agent? What is missing?"

Measures: task success (y/n), time to War Room, misreadings of the evidence (e.g. treating associations as audience demand or as a ranking), quoted feedback
verbatim, would-use rating 1–5 with reason. **Report only real sessions; do not paraphrase into endorsements.**

Results: _none collected yet._
